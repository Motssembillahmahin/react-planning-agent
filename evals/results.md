## Metric definitions

- **termination rate**: runs that stopped through the agent's own logic (answer, abstention, or one of its guards) rather than the baseline's 50-step safety cap or an internal error.
- **task success**: runs that terminated and whose status matches the task's expectation in `evals/tasks.yaml`; when the run ends in `success`, its answer must also match the expected answer.
- **correct abstention**: share of `unsolvable` tasks that ended with status `abstained`.
- **mean tokens**: deterministic estimates (~4 characters per token) of prompt + completion for every LLM call.
